# caqtl-snp-overlap-analysis

## Summary

Quantify enrichment of human liver caQTL ATAC-seq peaks near total-cholesterol GWAS SNPs by computing an overlap rate (proportion of SNPs with a peak within 10 kb), its variance across bootRanges block-bootstrap nulls, and a z-score separating the observed rate from the null.

## When to use

Use when testing whether observed genomic range overlaps (e.g., liver caQTL peaks vs. trait-associated SNPs) are significant, and you need a block-bootstrap null instead of naive shuffling.

## When NOT to use

- When the analysis goal is overlap counts of DEGs vs. differential accessibility peaks with spline-fitted logFC thresholds (use the macrophage.R workflow instead)
- When deriving mean correlation from Single Cell Multiome ATAC + Gene Expression data (use multiomicsCor.R instead)

## Inputs

- GRanges objects of human liver caQTL ATAC-seq peaks (from GRanges_construct.R preprocessing)
- GRanges objects of total cholesterol GWAS SNPs
- Block-bootstrapped null range sets generated by bootRanges (segBootranges.R), Lb = 300 kb and 600 kb

## Outputs

- Per-iteration overlap rates, across-replicate variance, and z-score in overlap_rate_zscore.tsv
- Bootstrap vs. shuffling z-scores across segmentation methods and block lengths in liver_chol_zscores.csv

## How to apply

Construct GRanges objects for liver caQTL peaks and total-cholesterol SNPs with GRanges_construct.R, then generate block-bootstrapped null range sets with bootRanges via segBootranges.R (block lengths Lb = 300 kb and 600 kb, segmented genome). In var.R, count per bootstrap iteration the SNPs overlapping a peak within a 10 kb window, divide by total SNPs to get the per-iteration overlap rate, pool the Lb rates, and compute their variance. Standardize the observed rate as a z-score = (observed rate − mean null rate) / SD of null rates, writing per-iteration rates, variance, and z-score to overlap_rate_zscore.tsv. Compare against a conventional shuffling null from bootstrap.R: block bootstrap gives z ≈ 4 while shuffling inflates it to z = 18.5, because shuffling yields an overly narrow null distribution.

## Related tools

- **bootRanges** (Generates block-bootstrapped null genomic ranges preserving local correlation structure, used to build the null distribution of overlap rates) — https://bioconductor.org/packages/nullranges
- **nullranges** (R/Bioconductor package hosting bootRanges, used with plyranges to load and intersect GRanges of peaks, SNPs, and null sets) — https://bioconductor.org/packages/nullranges

## Examples

```
Rscript var.R  # compute per-iteration overlap rates, variance, and z-score from bootRanges nulls -> overlap_rate_zscore.tsv
```

## Evaluation signals

- Per-iteration overlap rate equals count of SNPs with a peak overlap within 10 kb divided by total SNPs
- Variance is computed across the Lb pooled bootstrap null overlap rates for each block length (300 kb, 600 kb)
- Block-bootstrap z-score falls near ~4 across segmentation methods
- Conventional shuffling (bootstrap.R) z-score is markedly inflated near ~18.5, confirming an overly narrow null
- Outputs match the expected schemas: overlap_rate_zscore.tsv (rates, variance, z-score) and liver_chol_zscores.csv (z-scores by segmentation method and block length)

## Limitations

- Conventional shuffling or permutation schemes may produce overly narrow null distributions and over-estimate statistical significance (z = 18.5 vs. z ≈ 4)
- Results depend on the chosen segmentation method and block length Lb; both 300 kb and 600 kb were evaluated
