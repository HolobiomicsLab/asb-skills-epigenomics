# triplex-scoring-method-evaluation

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Benchmarks triplex (TPX) scoring strategies — thermal stability-derived measures such as the Normalised stability score against the match-count-based Triplexator potential — by computing per-lncRNA ROC/AUC over positive and matched negative regions. Grounded in the 3plex paper's finding that stability-derived scoring slightly outperforms simple match counts.

## When to use

Use when comparing TPX prediction scoring functions (e.g. stability-based vs match-count) on lncRNA peak sets with ROC/AUC, to decide whether biophysical scoring improves prediction over raw match counts.

## When NOT to use

- When the goal is simply predicting TPXs with a single default scoring scheme rather than comparing scoring strategies head-to-head
- When no experimentally validated positive regions (e.g. RADICL-seq peaks) are available to construct a positive/negative benchmark

## Inputs

- Top1000 peak sets (BED) for the 7 TPX-validated lncRNAs as positive regions
- Matched negative regions generated by bedtools shuffle preserving the positive length distribution
- Genome gaps and ENCODE blacklist regions (BED) for exclusion during negative generation
- ssRNA FASTA sequences of the lncRNAs and dsDNA multi-FASTA of target regions

## Outputs

- scoring_method_auc_comparison.csv with columns: lncRNA, scoring method, AUC
- ROC curves and AUC values per lncRNA per scoring method
- DeLong test p-values (roc.test) and BH-corrected Mann-Whitney p-values per lncRNA

## How to apply

Load the Top1000 RADICL-seq peak sets for the 7 TPX-validated lncRNAs as positives and generate length-matched negatives with bedtools shuffle, excluding positive regions, genome gaps and ENCODE blacklist regions. Score the same regions with each strategy: run 3plex/Triplexator with the Triplexator potential (match-count) scoring, then run 3plex with the Normalised stability scoring, defined as the maximal thermal stability among bedtools-merged overlapping TTSs divided by dsDNA length. Compute ROC curves and AUCs per lncRNA and per method with the pROC R package (version 1.17.0.1), compare methods per lncRNA with roc.test (two-sided DeLong's test), and assess positive-vs-negative score separation with a one-sided Mann-Whitney wilcox.test plus Benjamini-Hochberg correction (p.adjust, method 'BH'). Write the AUC table (lncRNA, scoring method, AUC) to scoring_method_auc_comparison.csv. Validation: the stability-derived Normalised stability scoring should attain an AUC at least as high as the match-count Triplexator potential, reproducing the reported average AUC increase of 0.01.

## Related tools

- **3plex** (Runs both the Triplexator potential (match-count) scoring and the Normalised stability scoring of TFO:TTS matches on the benchmark regions) — https://github.com/molinerisLab/3plex
- **Triplexator** (Reference match-count-based scoring implementation whose Triplexator potential score is compared against stability-derived measures)
- **bedtools** (Shuffles positives to generate matched negatives (preserving length distribution, excluding positives/gaps/blacklist) and merges overlapping TTSs for the Normalised stability score)
- **pROC** (R package (v1.17.0.1) used to compute ROC curves/AUCs and perform two-sided DeLong tests comparing scoring methods)

## Examples

```
Rscript -e 'library(pROC); r1<-roc(labels,score_stability); r2<-roc(labels,score_matchcount); print(c(auc(r1),auc(r2))); roc.test(r1,r2,method="delong")'
```

## Evaluation signals

- The Normalised stability scoring attains an AUC at least as high as the Triplexator potential scoring for the TPX-validated lncRNAs
- scoring_method_auc_comparison.csv has exactly the columns lncRNA, scoring method, AUC with one row per lncRNA-method pair
- roc.test (two-sided DeLong) per lncRNA does not show a significant AUC decrease for stability scoring relative to match-count scoring
- BH-corrected one-sided Mann-Whitney tests show significant positive-vs-negative score separation for the better method
- AUC increase of stability scoring over Triplexator potential averages around +0.01, matching the reported trend

## Limitations

- The advantage of stability-derived scoring is modest (average AUC increase of only 0.01 over match-count Triplexator potential)
- Only 2 of the 7 lncRNAs had IDR conservative peaks; the other 5 lacked replicate reproducibility, constraining the benchmark to Top1000 peak sets
- DeLong tests compare methods per lncRNA; no guarantee of improvement for every individual lncRNA
